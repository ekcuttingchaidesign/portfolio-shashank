Routed, not modal Real URL, back button closes it, page underneath holds its scroll position. A recruiter can send a colleague a link straight here. A plain modal loses all three — a quieter failure than the new tab you flagged, but a worse one.
Two templates, one shell Product case study runs nine chapters, craft episode runs six. Summary block, sticky rail, 64ch measure and breakout figures are shared, so the cost of adding a case study stays flat.
Gambetta on chapter openers Every opener is one line in italic carrying that chapter's takeaway. Rail plus openers = the whole argument in forty seconds, which is what makes a nine-minute read survivable. Assume 60% read the summary and leave.
