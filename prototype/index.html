Speed Over Silence: Designing Motion for Airtel’s UPI Payment Flow

Motion interaction

A UPI payment-confirmation animation built to make an unpredictable network wait feel instant. Ten motion directions explored, from encrypted light trails to a live circuit board, before landing on a road of procedurally-animated, faux-3D coins. Designed as an asymptotic, state-machine-driven Lottie sequence that can resolve to success at any moment, never stalling.

Experience · 2016 → now

No design degree 10 years anyway

Ten years, four places, one line through them: execution, then ownership, then a nought-to-one, then a practice of my own.

2016 — 17

MediaAgility

  1. 2016 — 17the way in

    MediaAgility

    Designer

    The first job that paid for design, a year after teaching myself the tools and walking out of engineering at TCS. Banners and creatives — small work, and the whole point was that the pivot had landed.

  2. 2017 — 18execution

    Appinventiv

    UI designer · agency

    Ecommerce, healthcare, B2B — many clients and a great many screens in a single year. Agencies teach you speed because they never give you time, and this is where the hands got quick.

  3. 2018 — 21ownership

    Gamezop

    First designer → design lead

    Employee-designer number one, and Skillclash built from nothing. The jump is not the title — it is going from executing a brief to owning a product with no design function anywhere around you to check it against.

  4. 2021 — nowlead

    Airtel

    Senior → Lead Product Designer

    Five years and five different jobs inside one company — two core products, a nought-to-one, and a practice I ran on my own.

    1. Wynk Music2022

      The streaming experience, and the flagship of that year.

    2. Airtel Gaming0 → 1

      Led it from nothing to launch. It was later shut down — and its design language is still running, because it migrated into Xstream and Wynk. That part outlived the product.

    3. Xstreamvideo

      Back into a core product, on the video side.

    4. The motion pod2024

      A solo practice: in-product motion as a discipline the org did not have yet, including the UPI payments work.

    5. IPTV2025 — now

      Current. The bundling work is this year's.
